# Building a Reproducible Air Quality and Health Data Workflow for Africa: A Data Management and Open Science Case Study

## Introduction
Air quality and health research in Africa depends on data from multiple organisations and platforms. However, datasets vary 
in terms of accessibility, documentation, metadata quality, formats, and licensing conditions. This project demonstrates a 
reproducible workflow for identifying, accessing, documenting, and preparing air quality and health datasets for analysis 
while applying research data management and open science principles.

## Data discovery and access
There are a number of open source air quality data available. However, for African countries, these are usually very few. The 
data exists but remains inaccessible. However, some accessible data sources and their methods (the ones i use at least) of 
access include the following:

| Source | Access method | Format | Metadata available | License | API
| ----------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| OpenAQ | API or direct download | csv/json | Sometimes | Open to use | Yes
| AirQo | API or direct download | csv/json | Yes | Open to use | Yes
| openAFRICA | Direct download | csv | Variable | Variable | Not evident
